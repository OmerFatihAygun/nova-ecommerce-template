## Notes on transitive dependencies

A scan of the installed dependency trees found only permissive licences (MIT, Apache-2.0,
ISC, BSD, BlueOak, CC0, 0BSD, OFL) apart from these items, all of which are build-time or
optional tooling that is not part of the code you ship:

* `lightningcss` — MPL-2.0 (Tailwind's CSS compiler; used at build time).
* `@img/sharp-libvips-*` — LGPL-3.0-or-later (native library for optional image
  optimisation; loaded dynamically, not modified).
* `node-forge` — BSD-3-Clause OR GPL-2.0 (dual-licensed, used under BSD-3-Clause).
* `caniuse-lite` — CC-BY-4.0 (browser-support data used by build tooling).
* `tosource` — the package metadata carries no licence field; it is a build-time
  dependency of `@nuxtjs/i18n` and is not shipped in the output.

Run `npm ls --all` in either project for the complete list.

## Demo content

* **Images** — every product, brand, category, banner and home-page image is original SVG
  artwork (gradients plus a line icon) generated by `tools/gen_placeholders.py`, created for this
  template by the author. They are free to use and replace. No stock photography and no
  third-party artwork is included.
* **Live demo photos** — the author's hosted live demo shows AI-generated preview photos in
  some image slots, for illustration only. They are not part of the download and carry no
  licence to you; the same slots in the download use the SVG art above.
* **Icons** — inline SVG written directly in the components; there is no icon font or
  icon-library dependency.
* **Trademarks** — the login page uses neutral icons (e-mail link, passkey, SMS code), not
  third-party logos. The storefront footer shows the Instagram, X and YouTube glyphs as
  links to those platforms' home pages; they are trademarks of their respective owners,
  shown only to point at where your own profiles go. Replace the links with your own
  profiles or remove the icons you do not use.
* **Legal pages** — the privacy, terms, consumer-rights and cookie texts are generic sample
  copy written for this template, not legal advice. Each page carries a "Sample text" notice;
  replace the text with documents reviewed by a lawyer for your business and delete the notice.
* **Data** — all names, brands, e-mail addresses, orders and reviews are fictional and
  generated from the seed files in `mock/seed/`.
* **Regions** — the state/province lists in `data/geo-regions.ts` are plain factual
  data (names and ISO 3166-2 codes) maintained in this repository.
